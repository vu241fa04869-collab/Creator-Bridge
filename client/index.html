04 — WHAT A RETAIL BUYER WILL ASK

Make the pilot easy to trust.

01 · PEOPLEWho is the creator?

Verify identity, audience geography, relevant examples, availability, and brand-safety fit.

02 · RIGHTSWhat can the brand use?

Agree on deliverables, disclosures, revisions, paid usage, licensing term, and approvals.

03 · PROOFHow will success be measured?

Confirm product links, inventory, baseline, attribution window, spend, and the source of each metric.

This prototype does not connect to Walmart systems or private retailer data. A real pilot would need approved product information, creator consent, tracking, and the retailer’s security and procurement review.
